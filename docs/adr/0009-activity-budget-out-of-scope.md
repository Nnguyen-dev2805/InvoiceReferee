# Activity budget is outside the current settlement decision

The current InvoiceReferee release does not require activity budget B and does not compare incurred cost T with B. Legacy budget fields and records may still be imported for compatibility, but they do not create FACT, POLICY, or AUTHORITY issues, alter the decision basis, or suppress a settlement effect. Activity identity remains the scope for evidence linking, money history, policy, and grants. The independent 8m cost-recognition, 4m automatic pay/collect, and 20m approver per-action limits remain in force; advances remain actual money received, never a budget.

The B-only D06 case is retained as future-scope material with its original source and oracle, but is excluded from the current generated denominator. The current authoring set therefore has 14 cases; the separate 15-root development gate remains unmet until an in-scope replacement is authored.
